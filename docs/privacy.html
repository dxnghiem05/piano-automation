PianoClip

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Overview

PianoClip is a private creator dashboard used to organize, review, and publish short-form piano performance clips owned by the app operator. The app is not intended as a public social network or a service for third-party users.

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to PianoClip and describes how PianoClip handles information in connection with its private creator workflow.

Information The App Uses

PianoClip may use authorization tokens from connected platforms, including TikTok and YouTube, only to perform actions requested by the app operator. TikTok authorization is used only after the creator signs in and grants permission.

The app may store local metadata such as clip filenames, captions, scheduled publish times, upload statuses, and video performance metrics. This information is used to manage the creator workflow and track which clips have been published.

When TikTok access is authorized, the app may read the creator's public TikTok video list for account review and publishing workflow purposes. The app does not collect TikTok data from unrelated third-party users.

Content

The app is used with piano performance videos owned or controlled by the app operator. It does not collect, publish, or sell content from unrelated third parties.

How Information Is Shared

Information is shared with connected platforms only when necessary to complete requested actions, such as publishing a selected video or reading video statistics. The app does not sell personal information.

PianoClip does not transfer TikTok user data to advertising networks, data brokers, or unrelated analytics services.

Data Retention

Local workflow data may be retained while the app is being used to manage publishing history and performance tracking. The app operator may delete local files, logs, credentials, and generated metadata at any time.

If TikTok authorization is revoked, the app will no longer use TikTok API access for that account. Local workflow records can be deleted by the app operator.

Third-Party User Data

PianoClip is operated for the creator's own account and does not offer public user registration. PianoClip does not request unrelated TikTok users to authorize the app and does not collect unrelated third-party TikTok account data.

Security

Platform credentials and authorization tokens should be stored privately and should not be committed to public repositories. Access to connected accounts should be limited to the app operator.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app operator through the public GitHub profile at github.com/dxnghiem05.
