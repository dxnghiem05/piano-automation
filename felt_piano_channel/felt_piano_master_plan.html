1. 🎯 The Opportunity

There is a proven, still-hungry lane on YouTube: soft/felt piano paired with an emotional, relatable title. The music is almost secondary — what drives everything is packaging that names a feeling and a moment ("it's 3am. let your heart rest.").

The core emotion the biggest videos sell is permission to rest — relief from the guilt of not doing enough. Demand for this is enormous and the supply that nails the emotional packaging is still thin. That gap is the opportunity.

Our specific edge: almost no one in this lane frames it through faith. The whole genre sells "permission to rest," but the secular leaders (panoir, Yuh Baek) can't credibly occupy a faith-and-rest identity. That is our open lane.

3. 🧠 What actually drives Views, Subs & Retention

👀 VIEWS — driven by packaging, not audio quality

  1. The title is a feeling, addressed to "you." Reads like a text from someone who cares.
  2. It names a specific moment / pain — "it's 3am," "overthinking minds," "emotional exhaustion."
  3. The core emotion is relief from productivity guilt ("you're not behind," "do not quit just rest").
  4. The thumbnail matches the mood, not the genre — dim, warm, intimate.
  5. Demand ≫ supply for well-packaged "music to feel okay to."

❤️ SUBSCRIBERS — driven by trust & consistency

  1. Parasocial consistency — same voice, style, and promise every time.
  2. Series identity — a recognizable suffix/format (panoir's "— piano session.").
  3. Low betrayal risk — no talking, no mid-video ads, no bait.
  4. Efficiency over volume (auteurs) — a few videos that deeply land beat 100 forgettable ones.
  5. Ecosystem pull — Spotify, sheet music/MIDI, memberships deepen the relationship.

⏱️ RETENTION — (inferred; exact % isn't public — see note)

  1. "One session" length is deliberate — 28–35 min = one study/wind-down block, long enough to bank watch-time, short enough to finish (lifts avg-% viewed). 1-hour+ chases raw watch-time (sleep autoplay).
  2. No talking, no dynamic spikes — nothing jars the listener into leaving.
  3. Background-first listening — sessions run long even at low active attention (VPH stays high: panoir 76–78, Yuh Baek up to 135 on a month-old upload).
  4. Title sets an intention the video fulfills — "let your mind slow" primes the viewer to stay.
  5. Loopability — no hard climax; runs into autoplay/repeat cleanly.

🔎 Honesty note: other channels' retention curves and VidIQ's outlier baseline are internal signals. Treat the retention points as well-supported inference from format + how hard the algorithm is clearly pushing these videos, not measured fact.

4. ✝️ Our Positioning — Faith-Framed Rest

One line: Lamp-lit felt piano that gives a tired, anxious heart permission to rest in God.

  • Why it wins: we get the genre's proven emotional hook without fighting panoir / Yuh Baek / REMEMBER ME head-on, and it's on-brand with the existing worship-piano channel.
  • The feeling we own: "you don't have to carry it tonight — He's got it." Faith reframes "permission to rest" as being held, which is warmer and stickier than the secular version.
  • Guardrails: keep it gentle and inclusive, never preachy or heavy. The music and mood lead; the faith is the framing, not a sermon. Scripture allusions in titles/descriptions are fine; keep them light.
  • Audience: Christians who study, pray, wind down, or fall asleep to music — plus the broader "calm piano" audience who'll still click a beautiful, warm thumbnail.

5. 🎬 Production Plan — the lamp-lit felt piano video

🎹 The sound (this is 80% of the product)

Two ways to get the felt tone:

A) Acoustic (if you have a real piano): add felt between hammers and strings — a practice-felt strip or a genuine una corda / soft-pedal feel — to get the muted, intimate, slightly-mechanical tone. Mic close with a stereo pair of small-diaphragm condensers (or a Zoom H5/H6). Keep the soft hammer/felt noise — it's part of the charm.

B) Digital + VST (best fit for a faceless weekly pipeline): weighted-key digital piano → a felt piano software instrument in a DAW. Record MIDI so you can re-render/fix freely.

  • Free VSTs: Spitfire LABS Soft Piano ⭐, Keyzone Classic, various Pianobook felt pianos.
  • Paid (worth it): Native Instruments Noire (felt preset) / Una Corda, Spitfire Felt Piano.
  • DAW: Reaper (cheap), GarageBand/Logic (Mac), or Ableton.

Mix / master targets (calm & consistent):

  • Short-to-medium plate or hall reverb for intimacy; gentle EQ (tame harsh highs above ~8–10k, clear low-mud).
  • Optional tape/vinyl noise layer + a soft rain bed — adds coziness and covers seams.
  • Light compression; master to ≈ −16 to −14 LUFS integrated, true peak < −1 dBTP. Mono-check.

Playing style: slow ~50–70 BPM, simple diatonic progressions, lots of sustain & space, soft dynamics, no big climaxes. Comfortable keys: C, G, D, F, Am, Em, Dm. Improvise long, uninterrupted takes.

🛋️ The look (your visual brand)

  • Room: dim and warm. One 2700K practical lamp as the key light, maybe fairy lights, a candle, a plant, and — if possible — a window with rain. Golden and moody, never bright/overhead.
  • Framing: pick ONE shot and keep it every week → (a) hands-on-keys close-up, (b) cozy wide corner with piano + lamp, or (c) over-the-shoulder. Consistency = brand.
  • Camera: phone or DSLR on a tripod, static or a very slow push. A still image with subtle motion (grain, gentle flicker, slow parallax) also works and is repeatable.
  • Signature: same warmth, same framing, same title font on every thumbnail.

🔁 Weekly recording workflow

  1. Record one 45–70 min improvised take (audio + a static visual rolling).
  2. Pull the best 28–32 min for the Session cut; use the full take for the Sleep cut (extend/loop to 60 min+).
  3. Light master the audio; add the optional rain/ambience bed.
  4. Build the thumbnail from the template (swap the title text).
  5. Export both cuts (see specs).
  6. Cut 3–6 vertical clips (45–60s of the prettiest moments) for TikTok/Shorts.
  7. Schedule the uploads.

💡 One recording session = 1 Session cut + 1 Sleep cut + several TikTok/Shorts clips. Maximum output per hour of playing.

📤 Export specs (ship two cuts from one take)

  • Session cut: 28–32 min — for study / meditation / wind-down (best avg-% retention).
  • Sleep cut: 60 min+ — for sleep / autoplay watch-time.
  • 1080p is plenty (audio is the product); 4K optional for a static/hi-res visual.

6. 📦 Packaging System

✍️ Title formula

[gentle faith-rest sentence] — soft piano for [tired minds / anxious hearts / rest & prayer / sleep]

Rules: lowercase, second person, name an emotion and a moment; keep faith light and warm; never reuse a competitor's exact line. Examples:

  • be still — He's got tonight
  • peace be with you — soft piano for an anxious heart
  • you don't have to carry it tonight

🖼️ Thumbnail system

  • One consistent lamp-lit, low-light treatment (piano + warm lamp, or a calm nature scene).
  • Same font, same placement every week; optional short affirmation text.
  • Aim for instant recognizability in a feed — the channel should look like one warm feeling.

📝 Description + tags template

  • Line 1: the emotional promise ("a quiet place to rest your heart tonight.").
  • Optional light scripture allusion.
  • Use-cases: study · prayer · sleep · anxiety relief · quiet time.
  • Links: (later) Spotify, other socials, playlist.
  • Hashtags: #softpiano #worshippiano #pianoforsleep #christianmusic #restinHim #feltpiano #calmpiano

🔖 Series identity

  • Pick a suffix and never break it — e.g. "— piano for rest."
  • Add seasonal tentpoles (like Yuh Baek's "goodnight, [year].").

7. 🗓️ Publishing & Cadence

  • Main upload: Sunday evening (~7–8pm local) — a natural wind-down / plan-the-week moment that matches the "rest" intent. Post the Session cut here.
  • Mid-week: post the Sleep cut ~Wednesday to capture the sleep/autoplay audience on a second day.
  • TikTok / Shorts: 3×/week (e.g., Mon / Wed / Fri) from the vertical clips, captioned with the same faith-rest sentence, driving back to the long-form.
  • Rhythm: one recording session per week → a predictable, sustainable output.

  • Views · Impressions CTR (target >4–5%) · Avg view duration & avg % viewed (retention) · Watch-time hours · Subs gained · Top traffic sources (browse / suggested / search) · Returning viewers.

  • 🟠 High CTR, low retention → fix the intro / audio consistency / length.
